01

Make your negative. Open a photo, shape the tones, then invert. Use the blue preview to get a feel for the print.

02

Find your sweet spot. Try a contact sheet with stepped contrast, exposure, or grain. Use a physical test print to choose your settings.

03

Bring it to the sun. Export a negative, print it on inkjet transparency at actual size, and place it against coated paper.

Proofs simulate tone only. They do not predict UV exposure time or calibrate your printer.
